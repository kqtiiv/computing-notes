Structure: Doing an action at a specific time | [Time] に [Verb] (Example: しちじに おきます - I wake up at 7 o'clock)
Structure: From [A] to [B] (Time or Place) | [A] から [B] まで (Example: くじから ごじまで - From 9 o'clock to 5 o'clock)
Structure: Going to a destination | [Place] へ いきます (Example: だいがくへ いきます - I go to the university)
Structure: Returning to a destination | [Place] へ かえります (Example: うちへ かえります - I return home)
Structure: Doing an action using a vehicle/transportation | [Vehicle] で [Verb] (Example: バスで いきます - I go by bus)
Structure: Doing an action with a person/companion | [Person] と [Verb] (Example: ともだちと べんきょうします - I study with a friend)
Structure: Doing an action to a direct object | [Object] を [Verb] (Example: ほんを よみます - I read a book)
Structure: Doing an action at a specific place | [Place] で [Verb] (Example: しょくどうで たべます - I eat at the cafeteria)
Structure: Inviting someone to do something (Won't you...?) | [Verb] ませんか (Example: いっしょに べんきょうしませんか - Won't you study together?)
Structure: Suggesting to do something together (Let's...) | [Verb] ましょう (Example: しょくどうで たべましょう - Let's eat at the cafeteria)
Structure: Doing an action using a tool, method, or language | [Tool/Language] で [Verb] (Example: えんぴつで かきます - I write with a pencil / にほんごで はなします - I speak in Japanese)
Structure: Giving something to someone | [Person (Receiver)] に [Object] を あげます (Example: ともだちに ほんを あげます - I give a book to a friend)
Structure: Receiving something from someone | [Person (Giver)] に/から [Object] を もらいます (Example: せんせいに ほんを もらいます - I receive a book from the teacher)
Structure: Borrowing something from someone | [Person (Lender)] に/から [Object] を かります (Example: ともだちに じしょを かります - I borrow a dictionary from a friend)
Structure: Describing a Noun with an Adjective (Affirmative) | [Noun] は [Adjective] です (Example: にほんごは おもしろいです - Japanese is interesting)
Structure: Describing a Noun with an I-Adjective (Negative) | [Noun] は [I-Adjective drop 'i'] くないです (Example: にほんごは むずかしくないです - Japanese is not difficult)
Structure: Describing a Noun with a Na-Adjective (Negative) | [Noun] は [Na-Adjective drop 'na'] じゃありません (Example: しょくどうは しずかじゃありません - The cafeteria is not quiet)
Structure: Putting an Adjective directly in front of a Noun | [I-Adjective] [Noun] OR [Na-Adjective + な] [Noun] (Example: おいしい りんご - a delicious apple / きれいな へや - a clean room)
Structure: Connecting two sentences with "but" | [Sentence 1] が、[Sentence 2] (Example: むずかしいですが、おもしろいです - It is difficult, but it is interesting)
Structure: Saying you "already" did an action | もう [Verb-ました] (Example: もう ひるごはんを たべました - I already ate lunch)
